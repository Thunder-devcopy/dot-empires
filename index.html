1v1 DOTS WAR

You rule the blue kingdom at the bottom. Destroy the orange castle at the top to win. The map is about 200 screens big, so scroll with WASD, the arrow keys or the screen edges, and use the minimap.

  • You can buy three things: Factories (people, building material or gold), Cities and Military.
  • Click a city to grow it: village, town, city, mega city. Barracks need a factory, airports need a town.
  • Turn-based strategy: you get 3 TP every turn. Building, upgrading, training, and issuing movement orders each cost 1 TP.
  • Press End Turn when you are done. The enemy then spends its 3 TP and both armies take their actions.
  • Dots conquer nearby grass during turn resolution. Water needs a Port or Airport.
